Company Overview

Driven by Precision, Focused on Client Success

Duas Unique Services Limited was incorporated on the 9th day of December, 2019 (RC: 1639974) with a clear mandate to engineering excellence, high-precision construction, and sustainable infrastructure development.

At Duas Unique Services Limited, we have built a sound portfolio of successfully completed projects across a comprehensive spectrum of sectors including Residential, Educational, Refurbishment, Hospitality, Commercial, Industrial, Healthcare, and Pharmaceutical infrastructure.

We have built our reputation on our client-focused and follow-up approach. We are proud of the fact that the majority of our workload comes from repeat business and that we have a strong reputation for our competitive edge and reliable execution of all projects undertaken.

This is achieved through our relentless attention to detail and an experienced team with the drive and customer focus to ensure client satisfaction is a given on all Duas Unique Services Limited projects.
